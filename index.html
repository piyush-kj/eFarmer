How it works

Three steps. That is all.

You do not need a computer or an agent. A phone and a photo of your crop are enough.

1

List your crop

Add the crop name, quantity in quintal and the price you expect. It takes two minutes.

2

Get dealer offers

Verified wholesalers near your district see your listing and contact you directly.

3

Sell and get paid

Agree on the rate, weigh at the mandi and receive payment. You keep the full amount.

Why farmers use us

Direct means more money in your pocket.

When a crop passes through three hands before the mill, each hand takes a cut. eFarmer removes those hands.

  • Prices are shown openly, per quintal, before you commit.
  • Every dealer on the platform is checked and marked verified.
  • You speak to the buyer yourself, so nothing is lost in translation.
  • Works offline once the page is loaded on your phone.
