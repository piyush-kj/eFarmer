Our story

Built for the farmer, not the middleman

A small idea: if a farmer and a dealer can see each other's price, both of them save money.

The problem

A wheat farmer in Madhya Pradesh usually sells through a commission agent. By the time the grain reaches the mill, two or three people have taken a cut, and the farmer rarely knows what the final buyer actually paid.

What we do

eFarmer is a simple noticeboard. Farmers post what they have and the price they want. Dealers post what they need. Both sides see the same numbers, and they talk to each other directly on the phone.

There is no commission, no subscription and no app to install. It runs in the browser on the cheapest smartphone.

Fair price

Open rates per quintal, visible before any deal is made.

Trust

Dealers are checked for GST and mandi licence before listing.

Simple

No login, no app, no English-only screens. Just a form and a list.

This is a demo project

eFarmer was built as a learning project with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Listings are stored in the browser, so nothing leaves your device.
